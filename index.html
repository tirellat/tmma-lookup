    192 elected members

    Winchester's Town Meeting is made up of 192 elected members, 24 from each of 8 precincts. Members serve staggered 3-year terms, with ~8 seats up for election each spring.

    Representative Town Meeting

    Winchester uses a Representative (not Open) Town Meeting. Only elected members may vote. Town Meeting meets in spring and fall to vote on the budget, bylaws, and other town business.

    Contact your members

    Members welcome constituent input before Town Meeting votes. Find contact details in the TMMA directory.
